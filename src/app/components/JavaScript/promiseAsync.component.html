Async and Promise

Promise makes simple implementation of callback and provides 'then' keyword to create callback chain

It used to perform a sequence of asynchronous operations one after the other but imapcted by delay of the execution.

ECMAScript 2017/ES8 introduced more simple way to implement Promise and Promise chain...

await pauses the async function at that point until the awaited promise settles. It does not delay the creation or execution of other promises that have already been started. Therefore, the order of await affects the order of result processing, while the timing of promise creation affects when the asynchronous work begins.
